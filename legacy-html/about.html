About Our NGO

HopeConnect is a fictional NGO identity used for this educational project. The proposed platform helps NGOs showcase their mission, manage volunteers and events, and connect with supporters.

Mission

Encourage participation in community welfare.

Vision

Make NGO outreach accessible and organized.

Project Goals

Centralize volunteer, event and donation-pledge information.

SDG Alignment

SDG 17: Partnerships for the Goals (primary). SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities and SDG 16: Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions (supporting).
